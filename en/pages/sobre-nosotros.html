Our history

Passion for Malaga
and for our guests

The Romero Luna apartments are located in the center of downtown from Malaga, in the gastronomic and leisure area par excellence of the city, 200 meters from Calle Larios, the Cathedral and the different museums that make our beloved Malaga famous.

If I say that we are really excited to welcome you, it is the truth. For us apartments are not only income, but also a way of life, meet people and share information about our beloved Malaga.

We are here to make your stay more pleasant. in a radius In a 5-minute walk you can go to both the best museums and places of interest in the city and to the best tapas or nightlife places. Cathedral, Picasso Museum, Thyssen, Customs, Glass Museum, Juan Breva Flamenco Museum... Malaga is a city that, due to its climate and people, She is very given to living on the street.
